WITNESS STATEMENT

Taken 18 September 2026

I live opposite and I park on the same stretch of road. I came back from a late
shift at about half eleven on the Monday night and the silver estate was already
parked in its usual place outside their house. I remember it because I had to go
round it to get to my space, and it was fine then.

When I left for work the next morning, just before seven, I noticed the front
corner of it was pushed in on the kerb side and the light was smashed. I did not
see or hear anything happen during the night. The road is narrow and there are
often vans coming through early.

I did not see any other vehicle involved and I cannot say when it happened.
