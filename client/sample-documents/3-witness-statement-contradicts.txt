WITNESS STATEMENT

Taken 18 September 2026

I live opposite and I know the car well — silver estate, always parked outside
their house.

On the Monday evening, about eight o'clock, I saw it being driven away down the
hill. I was putting the bins out and I remember thinking they were going out late.
I did not see it come back that night.

The next morning when I went to work it was parked up again with the front corner
damaged on the kerb side and the light broken. I could not say where the damage
happened. It was not damaged when it left, I am fairly sure of that, because I
watched it go past me.
